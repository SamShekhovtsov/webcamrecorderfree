# Web camera recorder free
## Recording laptop webcam surveillance system

this software is free to use, 
it was created for travelers to save life during travels.

I have noticed that my kidney is damaged in the night during travels in Asia and Africa.
That is why I have decided to build a software that can record laptop camera video
when I go sleeping.
This software runs on laptop and records laptop web camera video to *.avi files.
There are options for the video quality, for the recorded video files storage 
and an option for the split files size (chunk size).
This is surveillance software which is built to save peoples' life.

## Recording all night 10+ hours
## Saving to split video files with very compact compression and adequate size
## Optional video quality / resolution and split files size

### supported video resolutions:
 > (SD) 480p (640x480) - very compact, minimal video file size but low video quality
 > (HD) 720p(1280x720) 
 > Full HD (FHD) 1080p (1920x1080)
 > Ultra HD (UHD) 4K (3840x2160) - high resolution videos. Large video files.
 > 8K (7680x4320) - highest supported resolution, very large size of the video files

## Added new features:
- movement recognition - the software can recognize movement in the video and save only the video parts with movement, which can save a lot of storage space and make it easier to review the recorded videos.
- human recognition - the software can recognize humans in the video and after that recognize specific individuals.
- family memebers recognition - the software can recognize family members in the video and trigger an alarm if an unknown person is detected, which can enhance the security of the surveillance system.

## How to use:
You can run this simple software on your portable device with some lighting to protect your privacy.
If you fill pain or discomfort in the morning - you can review the recording and if there is something unusual - you should report to the local police department or make this accident public to avoid such ocassions and protect your privacy in the future.
As from my personal experience this might be very useful for travelers who stays in the hotels and hostels and communicates constantly with the foreigners. It can save your life, your health and protect your privacy.

## With the latest features here is the new flow:
- it constantly records the video from the portable device camera using very compact video codec and acceptable recognisable video resolution, which can save a lot of storage space and make it easier to review the recorded videos.
- it reacts to movements, can trigger an alarm if something is moving
- recognizes if the moving thing can be a human
- analyzes whether moving human is a family member or not, and if it is not - it triggers an alarm.
